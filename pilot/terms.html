Plurality Pilot Terms of Use

Plurality provides these sessions to invited researchers to test the pilot interfaces. Participation is voluntary. The study team provides any applicable research information and consent materials separately.

Using your session links

Your registration gives you three links for each of the two interfaces. Use one link per session. Save the links and keep them private so that activity can be associated with the correct pilot registration.

Using the AI interfaces

AI responses may contain errors, incomplete information, or bias. The pilot sessions are for research testing. Avoid entering names, contact details, or other sensitive personal information in the chat.

Contact
