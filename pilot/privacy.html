Plurality AI Pilot Study Privacy Policy

This notice describes how the research team handles data for the Plurality pilot registration and its email service. The study team provides participation and consent information separately.

Registration and pilot activity

When you register, Plurality asks for your name, institution or affiliation, and email address. You can also choose to provide your position or role. The service uses this information to manage pilot registrations and send you the session links assigned to you. It stores the registration with a pilot reference and keeps the corresponding sessions and recorded activity available to authorized study staff. A browser cookie lets the browser that registered return to its private results page.

Please keep your session links private and avoid entering identifying or sensitive personal information in the chat or other open text fields. Plurality sends chat messages to external AI providers to generate responses.

Gmail sending

Plurality uses the Gmail API to send a copy of your pilot links. The application requests permission to send email from the designated sender’s Google account. It does not request access to read, search, modify, or delete mailbox messages. Pilot participants do not have to connect a Google account.

Plurality supplies Gmail with your recipient email address and the invitation contents, which include your session links and pilot reference. The service records the send status and, when available, the Gmail message ID to track whether Gmail accepted the message. The sending credentials are kept on the server.

Plurality’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
